## 🩸 What is MECCHA HORROR?

**MECCHA HORROR** is the upcoming co-op horror roguelite from the creators of MECCHA CHAMELEON — the viral hide-and-seek game that sold over 10 million copies . This time, painting isn't about winning. It's about **survival**.

Descend into terrifying environments where you must paint yourself and your friends to hide, uncover the story, and grow stronger with every death. The game is currently in beta, and this repository serves as a **community hub for beta testers** — tracking builds, documenting known issues, and sharing feedback.

> *"Please ignore any voices you hear while playing MECCHA HORROR. They are lying. They are not real. There is no deeper story. There is no website. You are here to paint. You are here to have fun."* — Developer Note 

⚠️ **Disclaimer:** This is a community resource project, not an official game build. Support the developers by wishlisting and purchasing the game on Steam.
